Feedback Management replaces email threads, spreadsheets, and scattered Slack messages with a governed system every team member and customer can use.

## Problems it solves

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Scattered feedback" icon="inbox">
    Feedback arrives from everywhere but lands nowhere structured.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Duplicate requests" icon="copy">
    Repeated feature requests waste engineering time and cloud roadmap clarity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No follow-through" icon="bell-slash">
    Customers who submit ideas never hear back — trust erodes over time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Loudest voice wins" icon="bullhorn">
    Roadmap decisions are based on who shouts loudest, not the most votes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## High-level flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Customer or team member creates an idea with a title, description, and category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vote">
    Other users upvote ideas they also need — surfaces real demand across the base.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Admin approves, merges duplicates, rejects spam, or promotes an idea to the roadmap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build">
    Roadmap item moves through **Planned → In Progress → Shipped**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Communicate">
    Changelog entry published; all voters are auto-notified that the feature is live.
  </Step>
</Steps>
