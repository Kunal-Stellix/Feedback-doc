description: "Support agents can capture verbal or off-channel feedback from customers without exposing it publicly."

---

Not all feedback comes through the portal. Support agents regularly hear requests on calls, in tickets, or over email. Private ideas let that signal reach the PM without exposing internal context publicly.

## Private feedback flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agent submits a private idea">
    The agent submits an idea marked as **Private** — visible to internal team members only, never to the public.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add internal context">
    The agent adds an internal note with relevant details: customer name, MRR, verbatim quote from the conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="PM merges into a public idea">
    The PM finds a matching public idea and merges the private idea into it using the standard merge flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Association is preserved">
    The customer association is stored in the audit log. The public vote count on the target idea remains unchanged — private votes don't inflate public numbers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Private ideas never affect the public-facing vote count. They exist to give PMs qualitative context (customer name, MRR, direct quotes) without distorting quantitative demand signals.
</Note>

## Use cases

- Logging feature requests heard on sales or support calls
- Capturing feedback from high-value customers (track via MRR field)
- Preserving verbatim customer quotes for PM review
- Internally tracking requests before deciding whether to surface them publicly
