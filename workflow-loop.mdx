This is the core feedback loop — the full journey from an idea being approved to voters hearing the feature is live.

## Full delivery loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Promote to roadmap">
    PM promotes an approved idea to a roadmap item. Status becomes `Planned`. All followers are notified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start building">
    Engineering picks it up. Status moves to `In Progress`. All followers are notified again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ship the feature">
    Feature ships. Status moves to `Shipped`. All voters are notified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the changelog entry">
    PM creates a changelog entry — auto-populated from the roadmap item — and publishes it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the loop on the original idea">
    The original idea's status is updated to `Shipped`. A final notification is sent to every voter, completing the feedback loop.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Steps 4 and 5 can happen in either order. The changelog entry can be published before or after the idea status update.
</Note>

## Who gets notified and when

| Event | Notified |
|---|---|
| Idea promoted to `Planned` | All idea followers |
| Status moves to `In Progress` | All idea followers |
| Status moves to `Shipped` | All idea voters |
| Changelog entry published | Subscribers to changelog feed |
