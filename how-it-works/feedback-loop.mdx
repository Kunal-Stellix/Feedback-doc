The feedback loop is the core promise of the platform: every user who submits or votes on an idea will hear back when it ships. Here's how that loop completes.

## The full delivery loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Promote to roadmap">
    PM promotes an approved idea to a roadmap item. Status becomes `Planned`. All followers are notified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start building">
    Engineering picks it up. Status moves to `In Progress`. All followers are notified again — progress is visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ship the feature">
    Feature ships. Status moves to `Shipped`. All voters are notified — this is the moment the loop closes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the changelog entry">
    PM creates a changelog entry — auto-populated from the roadmap item — and publishes it. Subscribers to the changelog feed receive it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the loop on the original idea">
    The original idea's status is updated to `Shipped`. A final notification is sent to every voter, completing the feedback loop.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Steps 4 and 5 can happen in either order. The changelog entry can be published before or after the idea status update.
</Note>

## Who gets notified and when

| Event | Notified | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Idea promoted to `Planned` | All idea followers | In-app notification + email |
| Status moves to `In Progress` | All idea followers | In-app notification + email |
| Status moves to `Shipped` | All idea voters | In-app notification + email |
| Changelog entry published | Changelog subscribers | Email |

## The loop in reverse

What happens when things don't go perfectly:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="An idea is declined">
    The submitter receives a notification with the reason. The idea's status is set to `Declined` and becomes publicly visible with the explanation. No followers or voters are notified — only the submitter.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An idea is archived">
    The idea is quietly removed from public view. No notifications are sent. The idea is preserved in the admin view for audit purposes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An idea is merged into another">
    The submitter and all voters on the **source** idea receive a notification with a redirect link to the **target** idea. All votes and followers transfer automatically.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  The feedback loop is what turns a "black hole" suggestion box into a trust-building system. Every status change is a touchpoint with your customers.
</Tip>
