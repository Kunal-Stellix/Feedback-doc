Statuses are the backbone of the feedback loop. They determine who can see an idea, who gets notified, and what happens next.

## Full status reference

| Status | Meaning | Public? | Who gets notified |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Pending` | Submitted; awaiting admin review | No | — |
| `Under Review` | Team is evaluating it | Yes | Submitter only |
| `Planned` | Officially on the roadmap | Yes | All followers |
| `In Progress` | Being built | Yes | All followers |
| `Shipped` | Feature is live | Yes | All voters |
| `Declined` | Will not be built (reason shown) | Yes | Submitter only |
| `Archived` | Hidden, preserved internally | No | — |
| `Merged` | Absorbed into another idea | No | Submitter + voters (redirect) |

## Key distinctions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Followers vs. voters — what's the difference?">
    **Followers** are users who opted in to track an idea. They receive notifications at `Planned` and `In Progress` stages — these are the people who *care* about progress.

    **Voters** are users who upvoted an idea. They receive the final `Shipped` notification when the feature goes live — these are the people who *want* the feature.

    A user can be both a follower and a voter. In fact, submitters are automatically followed on their own idea.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Declined vs. Archived — when to use which?">
    Use **Declined** when you want to publicly communicate that the team won't build something. The decline reason is visible to the submitter and the community.

    Use **Archived** when you want to quietly remove an idea from public view. The idea is preserved for your internal audit trail, but no public explanation is shown.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when an idea is merged?">
    All votes and followers transfer from the source idea to the target. The source idea displays a redirect link pointing to the surviving idea. Both the submitter and original voters are notified with the redirect.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Notification timeline

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Submitter" icon="user">
    - Notified when their idea reaches **Under Review**.\
    - Notified if the idea is **Declined** (with reason).\
    - Notified if the idea is **Merged** (with redirect link).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Followers" icon="eye">
    - Notified when the idea reaches **Planned**.\
    - Notified when the idea reaches **In Progress**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Voters" icon="thumbs-up">
    - Notified when the idea reaches **Shipped**.\
    - This is the final notification that closes the feedback loop.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
