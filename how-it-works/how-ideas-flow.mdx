Every idea in the platform moves through a clear, predictable lifecycle. Understanding this flow is the key to understanding the entire product.

## The lifecycle at a glance

```
Pending → Under Review → Planned → In Progress → Shipped
```

<Note>
  From any status, an idea can also transition to **Declined**, **Archived**, or **Merged**. These are exit paths — once taken, the idea leaves the primary flow.
</Note>

## What happens at each stage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pending">
    A user submits an idea. It enters the review queue and is not yet visible to the public. Admins moderate and decide whether to approve it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Under Review">
    The team is actively evaluating the idea. It becomes publicly visible, and the original submitter is notified that their idea is being considered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Planned">
    The idea has been accepted and added to the roadmap. All followers are notified — this signals commitment to the user base.
  </Step>
  <Step title="In Progress">
    Engineering is actively building it. Followers receive another notification, reinforcing that progress is happening.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shipped">
    The feature is live. All voters are notified, and a changelog entry is (optionally) auto-created. The loop is closed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Exit statuses

Not every idea makes it through the full lifecycle. At any point, an admin can:

| Status | What it means | Visible publicly? |
|---|---|---|
| **Declined** | The team will not build it — a reason is shown publicly | Yes |
| **Archived** | Hidden from public view, preserved internally for audit | No |
| **Merged** | Absorbed into another idea; submitter and voters are redirected | No (redirect link shown) |

<Tip>
  Merging is the most common exit path for duplicates. See [Merge Duplicates](/guides/merge-duplicates) for the full walkthrough.
</Tip>
