Different people use Feedback Management for different reasons. Here's who they are and what they need.

## Personas at a glance

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="End User / Customer" icon="user">
    **Goal:** Have their voice heard.\
    **Actions:** Submit idea · Vote · Follow · Read changelog.\
    **Value:** Notified when their feature ships; trust builds over time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Support / CS Agent" icon="headset">
    **Goal:** Log verbal feedback quickly.\
    **Actions:** Submit private idea on behalf of customer · Add internal notes.\
    **Value:** Structured capture linked directly to the roadmap.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Product Manager" icon="chart-bar">
    **Goal:** Make data-backed decisions.\
    **Actions:** Review queue · Merge duplicates · Manage roadmap.\
    **Value:** Quantified demand; cleaner prioritization.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Workspace Admin" icon="shield-halved">
    **Goal:** Keep the platform clean and governed.\
    **Actions:** Configure boards · Moderate · Manage roles & integrations.\
    **Value:** Full control with audit trail and spam prevention.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Card title="Executive / Stakeholder" icon="presentation-screen">
  **Goal:** Understand top pain points.\
  **Actions:** View analytics · Read roadmap & changelog.\
  **Value:** High-level demand summary without the noise.
</Card>

## What each persona does

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Customers">
    1. Browse the public portal or embedded widget.\
    2. Submit a new idea or upvote an existing one.\
    3. Follow ideas they care about.\
    4. Receive notifications when ideas change status.\
    5. Read the changelog to see what's shipped.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Support Agents">
    1. Hear a feature request on a call or in a ticket.\
    2. Log it as a **private idea** with the customer's name and MRR.\
    3. PM finds a match and merges it into a public idea.\
    4. Customer is tracked without exposing internal context publicly.\
    See [Log Private Feedback](/guides/log-private-feedback) for the full walkthrough.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Product Managers">
    1. Review the ideas queue — sort by votes, newest, or category.\
    2. Merge duplicate submissions to keep vote counts accurate.\
    3. Approve, decline, or promote ideas to the roadmap.\
    4. Move roadmap items through **Planned → In Progress → Shipped**.\
    5. Publish changelog entries to close the loop with voters.\
    See [Merge Duplicates](/guides/merge-duplicates) and [The Feedback Loop](/how-it-works/feedback-loop).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Admins">
    1. Configure portal branding, custom domains, and moderation settings.\
    2. Manage API keys and webhook endpoints for integrations.\
    3. Moderate comments and flag inappropriate content.\
    4. Control user roles (user, moderator, admin).\
    See [Portal Settings & Branding](/api-reference/portal-settings) and [Admin & Moderation](/api-reference/administration).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
